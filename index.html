Academic & Research Projects

MedTech Image Classification Pipeline

Computer Vision Deep Learning PyTorch ConvNeXt

Context: End-to-end deep learning pipeline to classify 13 types of white blood cells for clinical diagnostics.

The Challenge: Severe class imbalance, clinical domain shift, and high colorimetric variations due to staining techniques.

The Architecture: Implemented a ConvNeXt model utilizing Transfer Learning, Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP), and CLAHE preprocessing. Mitigated data asymmetry using a WeightedRandomSampler, Focal Loss, and a 4-Fold Stratified Ensembling strategy.

Impact: Achieved a 0.7839 F1-Score Macro, securing a Top 5/77 ranking on the competitive leaderboard.

Unsupervised Abstract Art Clustering Engine

Machine Learning CNN (VGG16) Math & Statistics

Context: Multi-modal feature extraction and clustering pipeline to categorize abstract paintings without ground-truth labels.

The Challenge: Abstract art relies on textures rather than distinct spatial objects, limiting the effectiveness of standard object-detection CNNs.

The Architecture: Engineered a spatial-invariant texture extractor using VGG16 Gram matrices. Fused CNN, VAE, and Geometric embeddings via alpha-blending, and designed a custom "Core Silhouette" statistical algorithm to prune ambiguous K-Means clusters.

Impact: Delivered a complete evaluation pipeline (including t-SNE and distance matrices) with automated database scripts for web integration.
