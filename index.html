Academic & Research Projects

MedTech Image Classification Pipeline

Computer Vision Deep Learning PyTorch ConvNeXt

For this diagnostic imaging challenge, I had to classify 13 types of white blood cells from microscopy images, despite strong class imbalance and inconsistent staining across samples. I first tried a classical computer vision pipeline based on hand-engineered features, then moved to a deep learning approach built around a ConvNeXt model, which handled the variation and rare classes far better. The final model reached a macro F1-score of 0.7839, placing 5th out of 77 submissions.

Unsupervised Abstract Art Clustering Engine

Machine Learning CNN (VGG16) Math & Statistics

The goal was to sort a collection of abstract paintings into stylistic groups without any labeled data to guide us, a task made harder by the fact that abstract art relies on texture and color rather than the recognizable objects most computer vision models are built to detect. On this team project, I worked on the computer vision and clustering side: extracting style signatures with a CNN, fusing them with other feature spaces, and refining the resulting clusters with a custom filtering algorithm. The result was a full evaluation pipeline feeding into an interactive WebGL tool for exploring the paintings.
